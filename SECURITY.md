# Security policy

Please report vulnerabilities privately. Do not open a public issue.

- **Preferred:** [Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/strideaidev/extensions/security/advisories/new) (GitHub private vulnerability reporting).
- **E-mail:** legal@aistride.dev

Include the affected component (extension, pipeline task, website, platform at account.aistride.dev), steps to reproduce and the impact you see. We acknowledge reports within two business days, keep you informed, and do not take legal action against good-faith research that respects this policy, avoids privacy violations and service disruption, and gives us reasonable time to fix the issue before disclosure. Please do not test against other customers' Azure DevOps organizations or data.

The full security overview is at https://aistride.dev/legal/security/.
